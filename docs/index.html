Before you start

    
Staffie Sense reads visible body-language signals (how much your dog moves, how his body is held, sudden stillness). It does not read emotions, and the same signal can mean different things in different situations. Context matters.

    
It is not a safety or behaviour-assessment tool. Don't use it to decide whether your dog, or anyone near him, is safe in a situation.

    
This is an early proof of concept. It sees a box around the dog and the motion inside it. It can't yet see ears, tail, eyes or mouth.

    
Everything runs on this phone. No video or data is uploaded.
